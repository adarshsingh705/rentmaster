## 4. What Should Change in RentMaster Pricing

### The Free Plan Question: Should it be ₹99/month?

**Verdict: Yes, convert Free to a ₹99/month "Basic" plan. Here's why:**

- PGManager charges ₹300/month for similar features (no WhatsApp, no payments)
- TrackMyPG charges ₹299/month for fewer features (credit-limited WhatsApp, no payments)
- Your cost per 5-room user: ₹1.74 × 5 = **₹8.70/month in WhatsApp**
- At ₹99/month: gross margin = ₹90.30 per user (~91% margin)
- ₹99 is psychologically "almost free" — low friction, high conversion vs. ₹299 competitors

**However: You need a free acquisition hook.** Replace the free plan with a **14-day free trial**, no credit card required. This is what TrackMyPG does (7 days) and it works.

## 8. Should You Keep a Perpetual Free Plan?

**Arguments FOR keeping it (₹0 forever for 3 rooms):**
- Viral acquisition — small landlords tell their friends
- Zero competitors have a strong free tier
- Low actual cost (₹5.22/month for 3 rooms)
- Platform trust signal ("try it, no risk")

**Arguments FOR removing it (₹99 Basic instead):**
- ₹99 is still 3–4× cheaper than any competitor entry plan
- Earns money from every user
- "Free" users often never upgrade — dead weight
- 14-day trial achieves the same acquisition goal without permanent cost

**Recommended middle ground:** Keep a **permanently free "Nano" tier** — just 2 rooms, no WhatsApp automation, manual payment links only. This is a true "see the dashboard" tier, not a working product. Anyone who needs automation pays ₹99+.
