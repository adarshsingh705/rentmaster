FAQ
What counts as a slot?
1 PG bed = 1 slot (₹60). 1 flat room = 1 slot (₹60). 1 shop = 1 slot (₹80). Totals are summed across all properties. Residential and commercial tiers are calculated independently.
What happens when I add a property?
Adding a property increases your slot count and updates your plan. If within your mandate limit, it's automatic. If it exceeds the limit, you'll get a WhatsApp re-auth link. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle only.
Does RentMaster take a cut of rent?
No. Rent goes directly to your bank via Cashfree. RentMaster charges a flat per-slot fee only — completely separate from rent amounts.
Is electricity billing included?
Yes — free in all paid tiers. Choose sub-meter, flat-rate, or shared-split billing per property. Electricity amount is combined with rent in the same WhatsApp payment link.
