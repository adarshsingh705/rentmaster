Frequently Asked Questions
What counts as a bed vs a room?
A room is a unit with one tenant — typical for residential flats or independent rooms. A bed is a sleeping space inside a shared room, where 2–4 tenants split the room — common in PG accommodations. The Free plan supports room-based only (1 tenant per room, max 5 rooms). Starter and above unlock bed-based PG so you can assign multiple tenants to a single room.
When will Phase 4 billing launch and will I be forced to pay?
Phase 4 is planned after Phase 3 (digital agreements). You'll receive at least 60 days' notice before billing begins, and you'll never be charged without explicitly choosing a plan. Your data is safe regardless.
What happens if I exceed a plan's room or bed limit?
Existing tenants are never removed. If you hit a plan's room or bed ceiling, you'll be prompted to upgrade before adding new rooms or beds. Automated reminders continue uninterrupted for all existing tenants.
Does RentMaster take a cut of my rent collection?
No. Rent flows directly from your tenant to your bank account via Cashfree Beneficiary Transfer. RentMaster never holds or intermediates funds (NFR-14 — no RBI PA license required). Cashfree's standard processing fee (typically 1.5–2%) applies on each transaction.
Can I cancel at any time with no lock-in?
Yes. Monthly billing with no lock-in. Cancel anytime from this page and your plan downgrades at the end of the current billing cycle. All your tenant and payment data remains accessible for 6 months after cancellation.
What is the 40% early adopter discount?
All owners who joined during Phase 1 (before paid billing launches) get a 40% permanent discount on any plan — for life, as long as the subscription is kept active. For example, the Pro plan at ₹499/month would be ₹299/month for early adopters.
