Frequently Asked Questions
What counts as a slot?
1 PG bed = 1 slot at ₹60/month. 1 flat room = 1 slot at ₹60/month. 1 shop or office = 1 slot at ₹80/month. Slots are counted across all your properties — PG beds + flat rooms + shops combined for your total bill. Commercial and residential slots are tiered independently.
What happens when I add a new property?
When you add a property or room, the system recalculates your slot count and updates your subscription. If the new amount is within your auto-pay mandate limit, it updates silently — no action needed. If it exceeds the limit (for larger expansions), you'll get a WhatsApp link to re-authorize once. The new amount takes effect from your next billing cycle, not immediately.
Does RentMaster take a cut of my rent collection?
No. Rent flows directly from your tenant to your bank via Cashfree Beneficiary Transfer. RentMaster never holds funds. Cashfree's standard processing fee (~1.5–2%) applies per transaction. Your RentMaster subscription is a flat per-slot monthly fee — completely separate from rent amounts.
How does the UPI AutoPay mandate work for my subscription?
You authorize once via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm — takes 30 seconds. On the 5th of each month, Cashfree auto-debits your RentMaster subscription amount. You get a WhatsApp receipt. If the debit fails, you have a 7-day grace period to pay manually before the account is suspended. Cancel anytime from your UPI app or this page.
What is electricity billing and does it cost extra?
Electricity billing is included in all paid tiers at no extra cost. You choose a billing mode per property: sub-meter (you enter monthly readings, system calculates units × rate), flat-rate (fixed monthly amount per room), or shared split (total EB bill ÷ active tenants). The electricity amount is added to the rent record and included in the same payment link and WhatsApp message.
Can I cancel at any time with no lock-in?
Yes. Cancel the UPI AutoPay mandate from your UPI app or contact support. Your account remains active until the end of the current billing cycle. All tenant and payment data remains accessible for 6 months after cancellation.
